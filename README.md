# Hügelton Public License (HPL)

**Version 1.0 — Review Draft. Not yet adopted.**

HPL is a file-level reciprocal, source-available license for software, firmware, editable engineering designs, artwork and documentation. It permits internal business use, modification, gratuitous redistribution and independent outputs. Commercial distribution of the licensed material, paid access to its substantial functionality, and commercialization of products implementing a licensed design require separate permission.

The normative English text is in [LICENSE](LICENSE). [FAQ](FAQ.md) provides nonbinding examples. [REVIEW](REVIEW.md) records outstanding legal issues. HPL is not an OSI-approved open-source license.

After adoption, designate the covered files explicitly, include the license text, and use `SPDX-License-Identifier: LicenseRef-HPL-1.0`. For formats without reliable comment fields, use an adjacent notice or a manifest identifying the covered paths. Preserve independent third-party licenses. Previous grants remain unaffected.

Commercial licensing requests should be addressed to the relevant rightsholders or their authorized representative. A request or silence does not constitute permission.
