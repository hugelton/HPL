# FAQ — Nonbinding guidance

The English LICENSE text controls.

| Activity | Intended treatment |
|---|---|
| Internal business use and private modification | Permitted without publication |
| Selling an independently designed enclosure made with HPL CAD software | Permitted |
| Fabricating a board from HPL KiCad files for internal use | Permitted |
| Paying a board house to fabricate that board on your behalf | Permitted |
| Giving away a board made from HPL designs | Permitted with corresponding editable source and notices |
| Selling a board or kit made from HPL designs, including at cost | Separate permission required, subject to applicable rights and enforceability |
| Distributing Gerbers or flattened PDFs without editable source | Not sufficient where those files are not the preferred editable form |
| Free fork, binary distribution or mirror | Permitted with corresponding source and notices |
| Voluntary donations and sponsorship | Permitted if not consideration for access or features |
| Optional paid training or support | Permitted |
| Paid custom development for a client's internal use | Permitted with covered source delivered to client |
| Paid downloads, subscriptions or mandatory support | Separate permission required |
| Paid hosting of substantial functionality | Separate permission required |
| Free external hosting of a modified version | Permitted with corresponding source offer |
| Independently created paid plugins | Permitted subject to Section 3.4 |
| Independent implementation of an unprotected functional idea | Not restricted solely by HPL |

Source disclosure is owed to recipients (or relevant network users), not necessarily the general public. Private and internal modifications do not trigger publication. Independent components retain their own licenses.
